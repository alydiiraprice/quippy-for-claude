# Privacy Policy for Quippy

**Last updated: May 2026**

---

## Overview

Quippy is a Chrome extension that lets you add comments to your Claude chats. This privacy policy explains what data Quippy collects, how it is stored, and what it is not used for.

---

## What data Quippy stores

Quippy stores only the comments you write. Specifically:

- The text of your comments
- The colour you assigned to each comment
- The timestamp each comment was created
- The conversation ID and message position the comment is attached to

---

## Where your data is stored

All data is stored **locally on your device** using the Chrome browser's built-in `chrome.storage.local` API. This means:

- Your comments never leave your device
- Your comments are never transmitted to any server
- Your comments are not accessible to Quippy's developer or any third party
- Your comments are not linked to your Google account or any other account

---

## What data Quippy does not collect

Quippy does not collect, transmit, or store:

- Your Claude chat content or conversation history
- Your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
- Your IP address or location
- Any usage analytics or telemetry
- Any data about the websites you visit

---

## Third-party services

Quippy does not use any third-party services, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or external APIs. No data is sent anywhere.

---

## Data retention and deletion

Your comments persist on your device until you delete them within the extension or uninstall Quippy. Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data.

---

## Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

---

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a future version of Quippy, the updated date at the top of this page will reflect that. Continued use of the extension after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
